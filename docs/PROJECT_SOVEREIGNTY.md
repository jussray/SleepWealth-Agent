# Project Sovereignty Contract

SleepWealth must preserve its paper/simulation product meaning, practice-state durability, evidence semantics, and critical operating capability if an external provider, API, SDK, model, connector, runtime, sibling project, or vendor disappears or materially changes.

## Invariants

- **Human authority comes first.** Authorized humans are the root of consequential authority within the product's existing safety and money boundaries. Models, agents, providers, workflows, evidence, learned knowledge, fingerprints, proof cookies, configuration, and automations may inform or constrain a proposal but may not manufacture, renew, widen, or replay authority.
- **Human approval cannot enable real-money execution here.** The existing paper/simulation-only and no-real-money boundary remains stronger than this portable sovereignty contract unless a separately reviewed future product contract explicitly changes that boundary through its own safety, legal, authority, and proof process.
- Human authority does not rewrite reality. Approval can authorize an allowed action; only evidence can support a truthful execution or outcome claim.
- Project-owned contracts define canonical inputs, outputs, errors, authority, state, evidence, money-boundary semantics, and completion semantics.
- External providers and sibling projects are adapters/capabilities, not project identity.
- Critical dependencies are `REPLACEABLE`, `DEGRADED_FALLBACK`, `HARD_DEPENDENCY`, or `UNKNOWN`.
- Provider/model/cloud loss may reduce capability but must not erase durable practice-state meaning, money-boundary truth, evidence history, or recovery instructions.
- Provider swaps must not broaden broker eligibility, money authority, credentials, execution scope, retry semantics, or evidence requirements.
- Provider outcomes map into project-owned receipts; provider acceptance is not strategy success, financial outcome, or real-money execution proof.

## World as experience and knowledge

Legitimate real-world interaction may become evidence-bearing experience: paper/simulation results, deterministic tests, repository history, provider/runtime observations, market-data observations when legitimately available, experiments, operational failures, documents, and verified outcomes.

```text
World → observation → provenance → evidence classification → experience receipt → knowledge candidate → contradiction / red-team check → context + confidence + freshness → working knowledge → proposal → authorized paper/simulation action when applicable → outcome evidence → new experience
```

Experience is not automatically truth. One simulated result, model suggestion, paper trade, market observation, or provider response is not automatically a durable strategy rule. Working knowledge must remain provenance-backed, scoped, challengeable, freshness-aware, and supersedable.

## SleepWealth-specific boundary

World-derived knowledge may improve simulation, testing, risk understanding, practice-state durability, or paper-only decision support. It does not create brokerage credentials, real-money authority, increased risk ceilings, live execution, deposits, withdrawals, purchases, or financial commitments. `real_money=false` and `live_execution=false` remain load-bearing truth unless a separately authorized future architecture legitimately replaces them.

Presence in FCR continuity coverage grants no FCR portfolio, MCP, merge, deployment, provider, financial, brokerage, or execution authority.

## Mandatory audit questions

> If the current cloud, market-data source, model, broker sandbox, FCR, or another named dependency disappeared now, what stops, what survives, what degrades safely, and what evidence proves the answer?

> What experience is SleepWealth learning from, what would falsify the current conclusion, and which authorized human or local gate permits the next action inside the paper/simulation boundary?

Architecture docs are not failover, strategy, execution, or learning proof. Verify contract, money boundary, deterministic tests, provider adapter, practice-state recovery, runtime, authority, and evidence separately.

Use: Reality → ULTRATHINK → Red Team 1 → Lindy → L99 → OODA → smallest reversible implementation → focused proof → Red Team 2 / provider-loss + knowledge-corruption attack → Documentation Truth → rollback → next gate.
