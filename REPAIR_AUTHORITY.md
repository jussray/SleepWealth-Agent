# SleepWealth Repair Authority

SleepWealth may self-repair its software, evidence, simulation, and connectivity surfaces. Repair authority never converts a failing test, MCP caller, fingerprint, proof cookie, model output, or provider observation into authority to move money or take a financial position.

## Allowed repair authority

Trusted repository operators may repair source, tests, CI, MCP contracts/transports, non-secret provider adapters, validation, replay/idempotency controls, kill-switch logic, documentation, paper/simulation paths, and public/read-only market or provider probes on the existing carrier.

They may rerun failed GitHub jobs when diagnostically useful, repair stale evidence records, and verify UI/runtime behavior with Playwright where applicable.

Every repair must bind repository/base/head, provider/account/environment identity, capability/resource identity, failure fingerprint, evidence-only proof cookies, before state, rollback, and post-action readback. Identity movement expires inherited proof.

## Provider repair boundary

A trusted executor may repair non-financial provider connectivity/configuration when the action is reversible, exact-account/provider scoped, does not expose/rotate secrets, creates no spend or account ownership change, and cannot itself move funds or create a financial position.

Examples: repairing a health/read-only endpoint binding, provider capability metadata, retry/idempotency configuration, or a paper/simulation integration.

## Financial execution remains separately gated

This repair contract grants no standing authority to:

- buy, sell, swap, stake, transfer, or otherwise change a financial position;
- broadcast a real signed transaction merely because it is technically valid;
- initiate or complete a real payment, refund, withdrawal, deposit, or settlement;
- create a charge/customer payment request with real financial consequence;
- use or expose private keys, payment credentials, signing secrets, or recovery material;
- bypass the existing human-approval, eligible-adult, provider-session, amount/resource, idempotency, revocation, and kill-switch requirements.

The existing financial-action authority contract remains the only execution path for real-money/provider effects. A failure investigation may prepare a repair or simulation, but it must stop before real financial execution unless all separate current authority requirements are independently satisfied.

## Witness roles

GitHub/local tests may trigger source repair. Provider readback may trigger non-financial connectivity repair. Playwright verifies observable UI/runtime paths. Linear/Asana, Notion/Drive/docs, and Slack may repair their own record state only. No record system or model may mint financial authority.

Fingerprints and continuity/proof cookies remain non-secret evidence markers with `authorizes=false` semantics.

For consequential repairs report REALITY, WITNESSES, FIX, PROOF, RISK, ROLLBACK, NEXT GATE.
